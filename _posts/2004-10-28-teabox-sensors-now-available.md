**"Teabox & Sensors now available!"**

10/28/2004

We are now taking orders for the Teabox and our various sensors. You can find more info at:
[http://www.electrotap.com/teabox/](http://www.electrotap.com/teabox/)
and
[http://www.electrotap.com/sensors/](http://www.electrotap.com/sensors/)

We will also be presenting the Teabox on *Paper Session 12: Interactive Systems* at [ICMC](http://www.icmc2004.org/) next week. We will have a limited number of Teaboxes and sensors with us for sale - if you are going, please be sure to say hi!
