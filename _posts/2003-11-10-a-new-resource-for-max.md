**"A new resource for Max"**

11/10/2003

There is a new resource site for C74-based software at [http://node.horizonradar.com/](http://node.horizonradar.com/). It looks like it could become a great resource. There were a number of these in the past that floundered - in part, I think, because of the Antiorp/NN thing. Now that we seem to be past that; and because of the really open structure of this site I think it we could see something take off.

If you haven't checked it out yet, I highly encourage it.
 -Tim
