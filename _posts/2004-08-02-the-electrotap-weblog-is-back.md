**"The Electrotap Weblog is Back!"**

08/02/2004

The Electrotap Weblog is back in order and should see some new activity! It was dormant for several months, broken for a few more, and then taken offline. I've revised it, and hope to make it a more dynamic place than it was previously.

Unless I get some emails requesting otherwise, I am planning on turning *off* the ability to leave comments. Most comments in the previous incarnation of this weblog were left by spammers. So this will hopefully alleviate that problem without upsetting anyone.

Enjoy!
 -Tim
