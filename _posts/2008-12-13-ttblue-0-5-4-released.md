[TTBlue](http://ttblue.googlecode.com/) 0.5.4 has been released. A lot of significant changes have been taking place in TTBlue over the last few months. This is the version that is being used to build [Tap.Tools 3.0](http://shop.electrotap.com/products/taptools).

TTBlue is the DSP library used in the Jamoma Modular Framework, Jamoma Multicore Audio Graph Library, and Tap.Tools. To reflect the integral nature and relationship of TTBlue to the other Jamoma projects, we are now beginning to refer to it as the “JamomaDSP Library”. A sneak preview of the new icon is shown above.
