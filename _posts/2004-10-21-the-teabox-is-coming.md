**"The Teabox is Coming!"**

**10/21/2004

We are getting *very* close to the release of the Teabox! We have several units ready to go now, and will be taking pre-orders starting early next week (shipping will begin on November 10). Below is a picture of a Teabox.

I thought I would also include a picture of a unit in action. The picture below is a Teabox that is being tested. It is sitting on top of my RME Hammerfall Multiface. If you are interested in seeing one, I will be at the AES show next week and ICMC the week after that. We will also have some Teaboxes for sale at those conferences - so if you want to have one in your hands before November 10, that's the way to get it. Just drop a line if you are interested.
