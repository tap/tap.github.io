**"Silicon Prairie becomes "Electrotap""**

**09/30/2003

Fun fun fun! While this blog started its existence with the name Electrotap, it has been hosted on the Silicon Prairie Intermedia site. As you may be aware, Silicon Prairie has been my music and software publishing outlet. Available starting today, you can access all of the Silicon Prairie site / resources @ [http://www.electrotap.com/](http://www.electrotap.com/). The site will be starting a transition...

While it isn't ideal to have a name and domain change, it isn't really ideal to have an identity with a 10-syllable name either (what was I thinking?). Also, the reality is that I probably won't be in Kansas City forever either, and what happens when I don't live out on the prairie anymore? It will just seem silly perhaps. Hopefully the transition will be smooth - I've allowed for lots of time in the transition process...

The layout of the size wil be updated, and hopefully the resources will be better organized. Drop an email or leave a comment if you have any ideas as I begin the transition.

'til later,
 -Tim
