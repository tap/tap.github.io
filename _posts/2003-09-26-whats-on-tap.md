## The Electrotap Weblog

The Electrotap Weblog is a blog for clearing ideas, brainstorming, and reflecting on life - basically those things that don't lend themselves well to more formal areas of our website. If you have any thoughts or reactions to any of our blog entries, please feel free to drop us a line...

**"What's on tap?"**

**09/26/2003

Welcome to the new Blog I've started... This will be a clearing house for my brain on various topics - many of which are related to [Silicon Prairie](http://www.sp-intermedia.com/) , some of which aren't . So what's on tap**?

First, technical stuff I'm think about or hoping to get feedback from. This will include some of the current issues I'm working through with regards to Tap.Tools , Jade , the TeaBox, and other aspects of what I do at Silicon Prairie (my electronic publishing company).

Next up is more-or-less anything that , including reflections on music, and other things I find interesting.

I always accept it and encourage responses and comments!

Enjoy!
 -Tim
