**"ICMC Wrapped Up"**

**11/08/2004

Saturday was a bit of a whirlwind. We presented our paper on the Teabox. Some presentations had 30 minutes, but ours only had 20 minutes scheduled (I'm not sure how that distinction was made) so we felt a little rushed. It went well. Because of the scheduling, we also had to miss the morning and afternoon concerts, but we made the last concert - which was perhaps one of the best concerts all around.

It's nice to be home, and now we can prepare for our Teabox ship-date on Wednesday.
-Tim
