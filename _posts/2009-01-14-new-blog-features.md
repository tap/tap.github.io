While many of our recent site improvements have been happening under the hood, the improvements that we have launched today should hopefully make it easier for everyone to interact with the blog. Here’s a quick list of what is in this little site update:

	- new search engine
	- category listing of the entries (linked from the sidebar and each entry)
	- date listings, for example:
	[http://electrotap.net/blog/2003](http://electrotap.net/blog/2003)
	[http://electrotap.net/blog/2004/10](http://electrotap.net/blog/2004/10)
	[http://electrotap.net/blog/2006/05/06](http://electrotap.net/blog/2006/05/06)
	- moved all old blog entries into the new blog

Enjoy!
