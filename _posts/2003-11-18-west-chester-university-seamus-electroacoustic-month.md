**"West Chester University: SEAMUS Electroacoustic Month"**

11/18/2003

West Chester University presents a concert of new works in cooperation
with the Society of Electro-Acoustic Musicians in the United States
(SEAMUS). The program presents new music by Greg Wilder, Tom Flaherty,
Scott Wyatt, and Timothy Place. Guest
performers include Chris Bryan (cello) and Charles Fricker (marimba).
The concert is scheduled for Wednesday, November 19 at 8:30PM in Swope
Auditorium at the School of Music on the West Chester University
Campus. Admission is free.
