I'm a software engineering leader specializing in audio and DSP, currently building the next generation of automotive audio systems at [Garmin](https://www.garmin.com/) in collaboration with BMW.

Previously, I spent 15 years designing and creating real-time audio software for musicians, artists, and researchers at [Cycling '74](https://cycling74.com) and [Ableton](https://www.ableton.com). I founded [Electrotap](https://github.com/electrotap), created the open-source [Jamoma](https://jamoma.org) project, and earned a doctorate in electronic music from UMKC.

When I'm not writing code, I'm climbing rock and ice in the mountains, building furniture with hand tools, paddling whitewater, or riding thousands of miles on my bicycle.
